HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

Can SavRS download every social media video?

No. Platform rules, copyright and access restrictions apply. SavRS does not bypass private accounts, logins, DRM or platform protections. Use official download options where available.

Can I convert a video to MP3?

Yes. Select a video or audio file stored on your device. Conversion is performed in your browser. Use only content you own or are authorized to convert.

Why might a direct link not download?

The file host may block browser downloads, require authentication or prevent cross-origin access. Try the owner's official download option.

Is SavRS free?

The current starter tools are free to use. Hosting and third-party service limits may apply.
