GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can SavRS download every Instagram, Facebook or YouTube video?

No. Platform restrictions, copyright and access controls apply. This starter version does not bypass logins, private accounts, DRM or platform protections. Use each platform's official download or save feature where available.

Why might a direct file link not download?

The host may block cross-origin requests, require authentication, or prevent downloads. Try the file owner's official download link.

Is the website free?

This starter frontend can be hosted on a free static hosting plan. Hosting providers and third-party services may have usage limits or change their free tiers.
